Who it's for

Made for these moments

Whether you're stepping into a new cockpit for the first time or dusting off your licence — Hovercheck has you covered.

New type rating

First time on a new type?

Loading a new aircraft into Hovercheck before your first lesson lets you familiarise yourself with the sequence before you even touch the controls. Follow along step-by-step until the flows become second nature — and reference the exact POH wording from day one.

Currency refresher

Returning after time away?

Haven't flown in a few months? Working through the full checklist before your return flight helps rebuild muscle memory and confidence — ensuring nothing gets missed when it matters most. Take it at your own pace, on the ground, before you're in the seat.

Student pilots

Learning to fly?

Hovercheck gives students a clean, readable view of the full checklist — exactly as it appears in the POH — so you can study procedures between lessons. Arrive at each session already familiar with the sequence, and use it alongside your instructor until the flow is locked in.

Daily flying

Flying regularly?

Even experienced pilots benefit from a consistent, methodical checklist. Hovercheck keeps everything in one place — offline, always available, and quick to reset between flights. No paper to lose, no app to subscribe to. Just the checklist, every time.

Built by a
pilot, for
pilots.

Hovercheck exists because most existing tools for pilots are either locked behind subscriptions, poorly designed for cockpit use, or simply not built with real flying in mind. We think that's backwards.

Every tool we build is free for personal use, works offline, and is designed to be usable in direct sunlight on a kneeboard. No accounts, no trackers, no friction.

We're starting with checklists — parsed directly from the POH and kept up to date by the community. If you fly a type that isn't in the library yet, get in touch and we'll add it.
