Hovercheck exists because most existing tools for pilots are either locked behind subscriptions, poorly designed for cockpit use, or simply not built with real flying in mind. We think that's backwards.

Every tool we build is free for personal use, works offline, and is designed to be usable in direct sunlight on a kneeboard. No accounts, no trackers, no friction.

We're starting with checklists — parsed directly from the POH and kept up to date by the community. If you fly a type that isn't in the library yet, get in touch and we'll add it.
